## Print the markers

Print the sleeves the day before. **Actual size / 100%**. No fit-to-page, no scaling, no borderless. Black only, highest quality. The **100 mm** bar must measure 100 mm (±0.5 mm), or reprint. Cut the **205 mm** line, and 5 mm off the top and the bottom. Wrap snug on a **60 mm** dowel, bands horizontal, **TOP** up. Glue the **16.5 mm** flap and press the seam flat. R = C / 2π at three heights, to **0.1 mm**, on the cane row. Steps: [How to print the markers](PRINT_MARKERS.md).

[All sleeves](markers/all-sleeves.pdf). BAS01 [315](markers/BAS01.pdf), BAS02 [36E](markers/BAS02.pdf), CRC01 [459](markers/CRC01.pdf), CRC02 [6BA](markers/CRC02.pdf), CRC03 [8A3](markers/CRC03.pdf), CRC04 [952](markers/CRC04.pdf), CRC05 [DAC](markers/CRC05.pdf), CRC06 [EC5](markers/CRC06.pdf).

STN01, STN02, and STN03 are where you stand. They are not sleeved. Calibration reuses a cane. This test does not use a checkerboard.

## Kit

- [ ] One iPhone. Rear camera, zoom **1×**, held **landscape**. Same phone for every photo, including the calibration.
- [ ] A tape that reaches **8 m** clear. STN01 uses all of that 8 m.
- [ ] A laser, or a tape that reaches **10.5 m**.
- [ ] A non-stretch line marked at **4.000 m** against the tape.
- [ ] A centre peg. Six canes for the circle. Two canes for the baseline. One cane for the calibration if you do not reuse a circle cane.
- [ ] Sleeves from **Print the markers**, glued snug on a **60 mm** dowel. Matte. Each sleeve’s own R.
- [ ] A small spirit level. The phone pole (about **1 m**). This sheet and a pen.

Camera location off. Leave the app’s **Offset A (mm)** and **Offset B (mm)** at **0**.

## Set out

1. Peg in the lawn. Line on the peg, **4.000 m** to the stick axis. Plant **CRC01** nearest the baseline.
2. Each next cane is **4.000 m** from the peg and **4.000 m** from the one before: **CRC02**, **CRC03**, **CRC04**, **CRC05**, **CRC06**. Closing chord CRC06–CRC01 within **30 mm** of 4.000 m, or walk them again.
3. Diameters CRC01–CRC04 and CRC02–CRC05, tape level, axis to axis **8.000 m**. Both within **20 mm**. Then write radius σ **3 mm**. Outside those limits: walk the sticks again. Do not write a larger σ.
4. From the peg through CRC01, **4.30 m** is **BAS02** (about **0.30 m** past CRC01). Write the distance you taped.
5. Baseline square to that line. From BAS02 back toward the peg mark **3.00 m**. From that mark swing **5.00 m**, and from BAS02 swing **4.00 m**. Through that side point, **BAS01** is **8.000 m** from BAS02.
6. Tape BAS01–BAS02 **both ways**, sleeve to sleeve, level. Level each reading, then add that end’s measured R. The two axis lengths must agree within **10 mm**. The mean is the length you type. Do not average a pair that disagrees by more than 10 mm.
7. Laser the same baseline, sleeve to sleeve, same height. This is **CHK-BASE**. It is not typed into the app. Retake if the dot is more than half that sleeve’s R (about **15 mm**) off the middle. Level, then add R for each sleeved end.
8. Bubble every cane (CRC01–CRC06, BAS01, BAS02, CAL). Middle of the bubble, about **1°**. A 2° lean at 0.5 m sleeve height is **17 mm**. Write height to the middle of the paper. **0.5 m** is a good target. Tick plumb only when the bubble passed.

## Photos

**1×**, landscape, lens glass over the middle of the pole, pole bubbled, then shoot. Stay on the pole when you turn. A second photo is the same station. Do not step. If a listed mark is cut off, re-measure. Do not add a turn.

**Calibration, before any station.** One plumbed cane. Front of the phone about **4.000 m** from the paper (between **3 m** and **5 m**). Tape that, level, write the raw reading. Axis length is the reading plus that sleeve’s R. One still. Call it **CAL** on this sheet.

**STN01 — left. 8.00 m / 8.00 m.**

1. Both baseline ends, and CRC01, CRC02, CRC03, CRC04, CRC05. CRC06 does not fit.
2. Turn. Keep **BAS02**. Add CRC06.

**STN02 — right. 6.50 m from BAS01, 9.00 m from BAS02.** Outside the circle.

1. Both baseline ends. No circle stick between them.
2. Turn. Keep **BAS02**. Take CRC02, CRC01, CRC06, CRC03. **Do not take CRC05.** It sits behind CRC03.
3. **Do not photograph CRC04 from here.**

**STN03 — right. 10.50 m / 10.50 m.** Required. Two stations are not a finished test.

1. Both baseline ends, and CRC01, CRC02, CRC03.
2. Turn. Keep **BAS02**. Add CRC06, CRC05, CRC04.

Tape each station from the **pole** to the **sleeve**, level. Add R once, for the sleeve end only. Write the readings below. The app has no box for them.

## In the app

This build has no probe page. Shoot in **Camera.app**, then load with **Library**.

Before you leave the house, in Camera.app: **12 MP**, **HEIF Max** off, **48 MP** off, **ProRAW** off, location off.

1. Open Garden Planting. Tap the bottom-right menu button (**Open menu**). **Tools** → **New garden**, if this phone should not keep an older garden.
2. Menu → **Sign in** → **Sign in with Microsoft**. **Version** is the file name (`1` is `/Garden Survey/garden-v1.json`, shown on the path line).
3. Menu → **Baseline**. **End A** `BAS01`, **End B** `BAS02`. **Length (m)** is the tape mean after level and +R, not the laser. **Offset A (mm)** `0`, **Offset B (mm)** `0`. **Measure with** **Tape**. **Save baseline**. Do not swap the ends.
4. **+ Point**. The **Aiming preview** opens. Tap **Cancel**. In the **+ Point** dialogue: **Name** `Circle`, **Geometry** `Circle`, then **+ Item**. **Save**. There is no box for sleeve R, sleeve height, or plumb. They stay on this sheet.
5. For a station, **Cancel** the preview if it opens, set **Name** to `STN01` (then `STN02`, `STN03`, `CAL`), tap **+ Item**, then the **+** on the **Point** row (title **+ Point**).
6. On **Aiming preview**, do not use the shutter. **Take photo** is aim-only and has no lens data. Tap **Library**. Pick the Camera.app still at **Actual Size**.
7. Second still at the same station: shoot it in Camera.app without stepping, then **+ Photo** on that point, then **Library** again.
8. Tap the thumbnail. The screen is **Mark BAS01–BAS02**. Tap **A** on BAS01 and **B** on BAS02, then **Confirm**. **Target**, **Suggest**, **Clear**, and **Cancel** are the other buttons. There is no CRC button. Write which sticks were in the frame on this sheet.
9. The **Photo** row must read **verified**, and the line under it **Upload verified.** If it says **failed**, tap **Retry**. If the message is **Sign in to upload.**, menu → **Sign in** → **Sign in**. A line **Not usable for focal length.** means retake at 1× in Camera.app and pick **Library** at **Actual Size** again.
10. Menu → **Sign in** → **Save**. Wait until it says the file was saved to OneDrive. The path line is the file you send.

There is no left/right control. Tick the side on this sheet. Do not type 0 or 1. Do not type the laser, the check diameter, or the check chord.
